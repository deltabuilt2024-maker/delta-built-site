Environmental Consulting & Compliance

We provide comprehensive environmental consulting services that help organizations and projects understand and effectively meet applicable environmental requirements, regulations, and standards in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Request a consultationTalk to an expert

Overview

Our services cover environmental assessments, regulatory requirements identification, environmental studies and reports, permitting and licensing support, and coordination with relevant regulatory authorities. We also help clients establish practical environmental management procedures that strengthen compliance, minimize environmental and operational risks, and support business continuity. Our approach is tailored to the nature, scale, and operational stage of each project, transforming environmental compliance from a regulatory obligation into an effective management framework that protects the environment, reduces risk, and supports long-term business performance.

EIAEMPPermits & RecordsEnvironmental AuditCompliance

Challenges we solve

  • Uncertainty over which environmental requirements apply to an activity, and when each study is required.
  • Permit delays caused by missing baseline data or studies that fall short of reviewer expectations.
  • Difficulty turning permit conditions into day-to-day procedures on site and in operation.
  • Exposure to violations and penalties when environmental records and periodic audits are missing.

How we deliver

  1. 1

    Activity review and screening

    We review the project's nature, location and stage to define the studies and permits required and the scope of each.

  2. 2

    Baseline data and site visits

    We build the environmental baseline from site visits, measurements and reliable secondary sources.

  3. 3

    Assessment and analysis

    We identify environmental aspects and impacts, rate their significance, and define mitigation and monitoring.

  4. 4

    Report preparation and submission

    We prepare the study or plan to the applicable requirements and support submission and responses to review comments.

  5. 5

    Follow-up and compliance

    We help implement permit conditions, keep records current and run periodic audits across the project's life.

What you receive

  • An EIA or EMP matched to the activity's environmental classification
  • Register of environmental aspects, impacts and mitigation measures
  • Environmental monitoring programme with defined performance indicators
  • Environmental emergency and response procedures
  • Periodic audit reports with a corrective action plan
