Green Buildings & Sustainable Infrastructure

We support developers, asset owners, consultants, and contractors in delivering buildings and infrastructure projects with enhanced environmental performance and sustainability, integrating environmental considerations across the planning, design, construction, and operational stages.

Request a consultationTalk to an expert

Overview

We focus on improving energy and water efficiency, resource management, material selection, and indoor environmental quality. We also provide technical and advisory support for projects seeking to implement sustainable building practices and pursue relevant green building standards and certifications. Our approach considers buildings and infrastructure as integrated systems, balancing environmental performance, operational efficiency, economic value, and user needs throughout the project lifecycle.

LEEDMostadamWELLEnvision

Challenges we solve

  • Choosing the right rating system and target certification level.
  • Sustainability requirements arriving after design is fixed, which raises cost.
  • Documenting credits through design and construction with many parties involved.
  • Delivering operating performance that matches the design intent.

How we deliver

  1. 1

    Feasibility and system selection

    We compare rating systems and set the target level, achievable credits and their cost.

  2. 2

    Integrated design workshop

    We bring the project team together early to integrate energy, water, materials and indoor environment requirements.

  3. 3

    Design reviews

    We review drawings and specifications and track each credit's requirements.

  4. 4

    Construction follow-up

    We follow implementation on site and collect contractor and supplier documentation.

  5. 5

    Submission and certification

    We compile the submission and manage the certifying body's reviews through to certification.

What you receive

  • Feasibility report and target credit scorecard
  • Sustainability requirements for design and tender documents
  • Credit tracking log and documentation
  • Certification submission package
  • Operational recommendations to sustain performance
